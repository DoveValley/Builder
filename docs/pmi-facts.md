# PMI Facts — Verified Source Data for the PDU Content Cluster

Checked directly against PMI's own documents (not third-party blogs) on **2026-11-08**. Sources:

- **CCR Handbook** (current, "Last updated April 2026"): `https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/certifications/ccr-certification-requirements-handbook.pdf`
- **PMI Trademark Usage Guidelines** (Rev. Jan 2023): `https://www.pmi.org/-/media/pmi/documents/public/pdf/about/press-media/trademark-usage-guidelines-new.pdf`

Do not restate these numbers from memory in future work — re-check the CCR Handbook link above, since PMI revises it periodically (it carries its own "last updated" date on every page).

|---|---|---|---|---|

| PgMP | 3 yrs | 60 | 35 | 25 |
| PfMP | 3 yrs | 60 | 35 | 25 |
| PMI-PBA | 3 yrs | 60 | 35 | 25 |
| PMI-ACP | 3 yrs | 30 | 18 | 12 |
| PMI-RMP | 3 yrs | 30 | 18 | 12 |
| PMI-SP | 3 yrs | 30 | 18 | 12 |
| PMI-CP | 3 yrs | 30 | 18 | 12 |
| PMI-CPMAI | 3 yrs | 30 | 18 | 12 |
| **PMI-PMOCP** | 3 yrs | **30** | **20** | **10** |
| CAPM | 3 yrs | 15 | 9 | 6 |
| Disciplined Agile (DA) | **1 yr** | 7 (agile-topic PDUs) | — | — |

PMI-PMOCP is **30 total, not 20** — confirms the correction already made in the 10-31 cluster (see `[[project_granite_pdu_cluster]]`); the "20" some sources cite is actually just its Education-PDU floor, not the cycle total.

## Talent Triangle category minimums (within the Education PDU total)

| Credential (Education min) | Ways of Working | Power Skills | Business Acumen | Remaining (any area) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PMP/PgMP/PfMP/PMI-PBA (35) | 8 | 8 | 8 | 11 |
| PMI-ACP/PMI-CP/PMI-RMP/PMI-SP/PMI-CPMAI (18) | 4 | 4 | 4 | 6 |
| PMI-PMOCP (20) | 3 | 7 | 10 | 10 |
| CAPM (9) | 2 | 2 | 2 | 3 |

Education PDUs can exceed the minimum with no upper cap; only Giving Back has a hard ceiling.

## Giving Back activities (for PMP specifically — the brief's main audience)

- Overall cap: **25 PDUs max** per 3-year cycle (optional category — can be skipped entirely by earning 60 Education PDUs instead).
- **"Working as a Practitioner"** (working in a role related to the certification): capped at **8 PDUs** for PMP/PgMP/PfMP/PMI-PBA (4 for the 30-PDU tier, 2 for CAPM). This sub-cap counts against — not in addition to — the overall Giving Back max.
- Other Giving Back categories (Create Content, Give a Presentation, Share Knowledge, Volunteer) have no PMI-stated individual sub-caps beyond the overall 25.
- 1 PDU = 1 hour; fractional PDUs down to 0.25 (15 min) are allowed.

## Carryover

Up to **20 PDUs** (PMP/PgMP/PfMP/PMI-PBA), **10** (ACP/CP/CPMAI/PMOCP/RMP/SP), or **5** (CAPM) earned in the **final 12 months** of a cycle can carry into the next cycle. Only PDUs from that last year qualify — not the whole cycle.

## Renewal fee — ⚠ NOT independently verifiable as a fixed number

The current CCR Handbook **deliberately does not publish a dollar figure.** Its exact wording: *"Renewal fees are subject to membership and regional pricing. For the most up-to-date pricing, log into your mypmi dashboard... and click Renew Certification."* Also clarifies PMI's **membership** renewal fee is separate from the **certification/CCR** renewal fee — don't conflate the two in copy.

Third-party sites widely cite **$60 (member) / $150 (non-member)** for PMP renewal, but this is **not confirmed from the primary source** here and may be stale or region-specific. **Do not publish a specific dollar figure as PMI's fee without a live check at `my.pmi.org`** at copy-writing time, or phrase it as a range with a pointer to PMI's own dashboard for the exact current number.

## Suspension / expiration

- Miss the cycle-end deadline → **Suspended status for 1 year (12 months)**. Cannot call yourself a "certification holder" or use the credential designation during suspension; next cycle's clock doesn't move, it overlaps the suspension period.
- Suspension completed in time → reinstated to Active.
- Suspension period lapses without completing renewal → **Expired status**: can no longer submit PDUs, must reapply from scratch — **new application, new fees, and retake the exam.**

## Audit

- A **percentage of certification holders are randomly selected** — no published audit rate. Don't state a specific percentage anywhere; PMI doesn't disclose one.
- If selected, renewal is only completed after the audit is passed.
- Keep PDU documentation for **at least 18 months** after the cycle ends.

## CCRS reporting — ⚠ affects the $299 tier's core promise directly

The Handbook consistently frames PDU reporting as the **certificant's own responsibility**: *"You are responsible for reporting your PDU activities... Report PDUs in CCRS by going to your PMI dashboard and choosing 'report PDUs.'"* The only auto-reporting mechanism the Handbook names is a **direct account link between ProjectManagement.com and the certificant's own PMI.org account** — a specific platform integration, not a general capability available to any ATP.

**Nothing in the Handbook describes a mechanism for a training provider to log into CCRS and submit PDUs on a student's behalf.** That would also require the student to hand over their own PMI login — not something PMI's documented flow supports.

**This means the brief's $299 tier promise, "Granite reports your PDUs to CCRS for you," is very likely not literally deliverable as written** — I found no evidence PMI lets an ATP do that. What Granite probably *can* credibly offer: pre-filled certificates of completion with provider name, course title, dates, and the Talent Triangle PDU breakdown already itemized, so the student's own CCRS entry takes under a minute instead of requiring them to track it all down — plus possibly guided/concierge reporting support. **Flagging to Scott rather than guessing further (per the brief's own Rule 1): confirm directly with PMI's ATP Program team (ATP Resource Center / ATP Hub) whether any provider-side reporting capability exists before finalizing the $299 tier's wording.** Until that's confirmed, the $299 tier copy should NOT state "we report it for you" — use "we make reporting fast" / "pre-filled documentation" language instead.

## Trademark usage (PMI Trademark Usage Guidelines, Rev. Jan 2023 + confirmed live in the current CCR Handbook body text)

- **® appears on marks PMI has actually registered** (per PMI's own "List of Marks," which this guide points to but doesn't reproduce in full). Directly observed in the current, April-2026 CCR Handbook's own body text: **"PMI Talent Triangle®"** is used with ® live, twice — treat it as registered, not ™, despite some older third-party guides suggesting ™.
- **PMBOK is a special case**: always italicized *and* always carries ® on every occurrence (not just first use) — *PMBOK® Guide*, never "PMBOK Guide" or "PMBOK®Guide" without the italics.
- **First-use rule for everything else**: the ® (or ™/SM) only needs to appear at first mention in a document/page, not every subsequent occurrence.
- **Use as adjective + generic noun, never as a standalone noun or plural**: "PMP® certification," "PMP® certification holders" — never "a PMP" or "PMPs." The guide explicitly calls out "PMPs" as incorrect.
- **The PMP Certification Logo** (the actual badge/seal image) may **only** be used by individual PMP holders in good standing, on their own business card or signature block — **Granite as a company cannot display this logo on its own marketing pages.** The separate "PMI Authorized Training Partner" logo is a different asset, usable under the ATP agreement, year-dated to show active status — pull the current-year version from the ATP Marketing Portal (`marketing.pmi.org`), don't reuse an old one.
- **Attribution statement required** at or near first mention, worded differently depending on whether all marks listed are registered or mixed: if mixing registered + unregistered, drop the word "registered" entirely, e.g. *"PMP," "PMI Talent Triangle," and "CAPM" are marks of Project Management Institute, Inc.* — matches the pattern PMI's own Handbook uses in its footer.
- **PMI marks cannot be incorporated into Granite's own product names/URLs** — e.g. a product can be *described* as "covers PMP® certification renewal requirements" but the product's own name shouldn't itself be built out of a PMI mark as if it were Granite's trademark.
